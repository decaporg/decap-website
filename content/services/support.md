Decap CMS is community-supported, and that stays free: ask in [Discord](/chat), open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/decaporg/decap-cms/issues), or start a [discussion](https://github.com/decaporg/decap-cms/discussions). None of it requires a paid plan — see [Community](/community/) for every channel.

What community channels can't offer is a commitment: that someone will look at your problem, and by when. That is what [Decap Turbo](/turbo/) adds as a paid option — standard email support on Pro, and a priority support add-on that is included on Enterprise. It is a support channel alongside the community ones, not instead of them.
