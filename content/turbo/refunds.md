## 1. Free plan

The Free plan has no payment associated with it, so there's nothing to refund.

## 2. Paid plans and add-ons

Pro and Enterprise plans, along with any add-ons, are billed via [Paddle.com](https://www.paddle.com), our reseller and merchant of record for all purchases. Paddle handles billing, taxes, and refunds for every transaction.

## 3. Refund policy

All refunds are governed entirely by [Paddle's Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy). We don't apply any additional conditions, review, or discretion on top of that policy — Paddle determines refund eligibility and processes all refunds directly.

## 4. How to request a refund

Request a refund directly through [Paddle.net](https://paddle.net) or by contacting [Paddle support](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy), which handles the transaction as merchant of record. You can also reach us via [our contact page](/contact/?topic=billing) if you need help locating your transaction, and we'll point you to the right place.

## 5. Downgrades and cancellations

Downgrading or canceling takes effect at the end of your current billing period; we don't prorate mid-cycle downgrades. Canceling stops future charges but doesn't retroactively refund the current period — any refund for the current period is subject to Paddle's Refund Policy linked above.

## 6. Contact

Questions about a charge or this policy? [Contact us](/contact/?topic=billing).
