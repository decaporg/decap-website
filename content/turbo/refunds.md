## 1. Free plan

The Free plan has no payment associated with it, so there's nothing to refund.

## 2. Paid plans and add-ons

Pro and Enterprise plans, along with any add-ons, are billed monthly in advance via
[Paddle.com](https://www.paddle.com), our reseller and merchant of record.

## 3. Refund eligibility

If you're not satisfied with a charge, contact us within 14 days of the charge date and we'll
review your request. Refunds are issued at our discretion and are processed by Paddle as the
merchant of record.

## 4. How to request a refund

Email us via [our contact page](/turbo/contact/?topic=billing) with your account email and the
charge you're asking about.

## 5. Downgrades and cancellations

Downgrading or canceling takes effect at the end of your current billing period; we don't
prorate mid-cycle downgrades. Canceling stops future charges but doesn't retroactively refund
the current period unless it qualifies under the eligibility window above.

## 6. Contact

Questions about a charge or this policy? [Contact us](/turbo/contact/?topic=billing).
