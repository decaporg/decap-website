  Saving tells an editor the change was committed, not that the site has rebuilt. Decap watches the deploy and tells them when their change is live — wherever they are in the CMS — with a Deploys page showing what shipped, where it went, and why a build failed. Deploy previews of entries in the editorial workflow are listed too, so you can see whether an entry in review has a working preview. Available for GitHub-hosted sites today; GitLab support is on the roadmap.
